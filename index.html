How it works

Five workflows: three for recruitment that hand data to each other through Google Sheets and Drive, and two that power the HR assistant.

1

Intake

Form trigger · 11 nodes

A public form collects the application. Bad submissions are sent back with clear fixes, duplicates are caught, and good ones are saved.

Apply form→ Validate→ Duplicate check→ Upload CV to Drive→ Log in Sheet→ Thank you page
2

AI Screening

Every hour · 30 nodes

Reads new applications one by one, extracts the CV text (with Gemini OCR for scanned PDFs), builds a candidate profile and scores the fit. The result is validated before anything is sent.

New applications→ Download CV→ PDF readable?→ Gemini OCR→ Extract profile→ Score candidate→ Validate→ Decision
InterviewInvite sent to the candidate and a brief goes to HR.
MaybeFlagged for HR with the evaluation to decide.
RejectA polite decline email is sent.
AI output invalid→ Marked for manual review→ HR notified
3

Error Handler

Error + schedules · 18 nodes

Watches the other workflows. It logs failures, emails the admin without spamming, sends a daily digest, and checks that the queue keeps moving.

On workflow error→ Classify→ Throttle check→ Log to sheet→ Alert admin
Every morning 8 AM→Daily digest   |   Every 2 hours→Stuck applications?→Heartbeat alert
4

HR Policy Assistant (RAG)

Chat trigger · 6 nodes

A public chat that searches the policy knowledge base first and answers only from what it finds. It names the source, replies in the language of the question, and sends the user to HR when nothing matches.

Employee question→ AI agent (Groq)→ Search policies (Gemini embeddings)→ Answer with source
5

Policy Loader

Start, daily, manual · 9 nodes

Keeps the knowledge base permanent. It reloads the policy documents from Google Drive when n8n starts, every morning at 7, or on demand. Editing a Google Doc is enough to update the assistant.

Start / 7 AM / manual→ List files in Drive→ Download as text→ Split + embed→ Knowledge base
