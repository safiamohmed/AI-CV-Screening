    HR Assistant

    How it works

    Five workflows. Three handle recruitment and pass data through Google Sheets and Drive. Two power the HR assistant.

    Intake form trigger · 11 nodes

    A public form collects the application. Bad submissions come back with clear fixes, duplicates are caught, good ones are saved.

    Apply form→Validate→Duplicate check→Upload CV to Drive→Log in Sheet→Thank you page

    AI Screening every hour · 30 nodes

    Reads new applications one by one, extracts the CV text (Gemini OCR for scanned PDFs), builds a candidate profile and scores the fit. The result is validated before anything is sent.

    New applications→Download CV→PDF readable?→Gemini OCR→Extract profile→Score candidate→Validate→Decision
    InterviewInvite sent to the candidate, brief to HR.
    MaybeFlagged for HR with the evaluation.
    RejectA polite decline email is sent.
    AI output invalid→Marked for manual review→HR notified

    Error Handler error + schedules · 18 nodes

    Watches the other workflows. It logs failures, emails the admin without spamming, sends a daily digest and checks that the queue keeps moving.

    On workflow error→Classify→Throttle check→Log to sheet→Alert admin
    8 AM→Daily digest | Every 2 hours→Stuck applications?→Heartbeat alert

    HR Policy Assistant chat trigger · 6 nodes

    Searches the policy knowledge base first and answers only from what it finds. It names the source, replies in the language of the question and sends the user to HR when nothing matches.

    Employee question→AI agent (Groq)→Search policies (Gemini embeddings)→Answer with source

    Policy Loader start, daily, manual · 9 nodes

    Keeps the knowledge base current. It reloads the policy documents from Google Drive when n8n starts, every morning at 7, or on demand. Editing a Google Doc is enough to update the assistant.

    Start / 7 AM / manual→List files in Drive→Download as text→Split + embed→Knowledge base
